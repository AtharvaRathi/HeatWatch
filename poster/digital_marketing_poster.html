1. INTRODUCTION
  • Background of the problem: Rising global temperatures require effective communication of heatwave alerts to the public.
  • Motivation for the study: Bridging the gap between AI-driven climate intelligence (ThermaGuard) and public awareness.
  • Relevance to real-world Problem: Essential for real-time digital dissemination to save lives and optimize resource allocation during extreme heat events.
2. PROBLEM STATEMENT
  • Problem addressed: The general public often ignores or remains unaware of critical heatwave warnings due to poor, unoptimized digital outreach and lack of localized targeting.
  • Scope and boundaries: Formulating a comprehensive digital marketing campaign for our AI-based Heatwave platform across web and social channels (Meta, Google Ads).
3. OBJECTIVES
  • Objective 1: Achieve 10,000 active monthly users on the Heatwave Alert website.
  • Objective 2: Maximize engagement through SEO and targeted social media campaigns.
  • Objective 3: Ensure 99% uptime of real-time digital alert broadcasts to vulnerable demographics.
4. LITERATURE REVIEW
  • Based on Marketing Strategy: Analyzed similar climate alert systems (e.g., IMD, AccuWeather).
  • Smith et al. (2022): Key finding — Gamification and real-time push notifications increase user retention by 40%.
  • Doe & Lee (2023): Key finding — Social media integration is critical for disaster management visibility.
  • Research gap identified: Lack of localized, AI-driven targeted ads tailored for specific vulnerable demographics.

8. KEY FINDINGS
  • Finding 1: SEO optimization on local climate keywords significantly reduces Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC).
  • Finding 2: Users strongly prefer color-coded, visual alert dashboards over text-heavy traditional reports.
  • Finding 3: Instagram is highly effective for reaching youth demographics, whereas Facebook captures older populations efficiently.
9. CONCLUSION
  • Summarize outcomes: A robust digital marketing strategy is essential to maximize the real-world impact of the Heatwave Prediction Platform.
  • Impact / significance: Proper digital channels transform technical climate data into actionable, life-saving information for the masses.
10. FUTURE SCOPE
  • Potential extensions: Multilingual digital ad campaigns, WhatsApp bot integrations, and Influencer marketing.
  • Limitations to address: Budget constraints for paid advertising; overcoming banner blindness and ad-blockers.
